Help with the Nur prayer app for iPhone

If something isn't working, or you have an idea for the app, email nurprayer.support@gmail.com. Messages go straight to the developer. It helps to mention your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

My prayer times don't match my mosque

Mosques follow different calculation methods, and the method decides how early Fajr and how late Isha fall. Tap the sliders icon at the bottom of the main screen, then choose the Method your mosque uses. If you follow the Hanafi school, set Asr Method to Hanafi as well.

A difference of a minute or two is normal. Many mosques round their timetables or add a few minutes as a precaution, so a published timetable can sit slightly later than the calculated time.

Reminders aren't arriving

First, check that reminders are turned on in the app. Tap the bell icon on the main screen and make sure Enable Notifications is on, along with the prayers you want. Then open the iPhone Settings app, go to Notifications, then Nur Prayer, and check that notifications are allowed there too.

A Focus such as Sleep or Do Not Disturb can hold reminders back, so you may want to allow Nur Prayer in your Focus settings. Nur schedules reminders several days ahead and refreshes them in the background, which works best with Background App Refresh turned on in Settings, under General. Opening the app every so often also keeps them up to date.

Snoozing a reminder

When a reminder arrives, press and hold it, then choose Snooze 5 min or Snooze 15 min. You'll get the same reminder again after that time. If you'd rather not see the snooze buttons, turn off Allow Snooze from the bell icon in the app.

The Qibla screen doesn't light up

Hold your iPhone flat, face up, and turn slowly. When you face the Qibla, the screen glows green, Facing Kaaba appears and you feel a gentle tap.

The compass needs a clear reading, so move away from metal objects, magnets and chargers, and take off any case or accessory with magnets in it. If it still doesn't light up, move your iPhone in a slow figure of eight a few times to recalibrate the compass.

Using Nur without sharing your location

You don't need to share your location. Tap the location icon on the main screen and start typing the name of your city. Suggestions come from a list built into the app, so nothing you type leaves your iPhone. For smaller towns, tap Search more places online to look them up with Apple Maps. The Qibla finder still works when you choose a city this way.

To go back to your current location later, open the same screen and tap Use GPS.

Your privacy

Nur has no accounts, ads or analytics, and your settings stay on your iPhone. You can read exactly what the app does with your information in the privacy policy. Deleting the app removes everything it has stored.
