Nur

Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Nur does not collect, store, or have access to your personal information. Prayer times and the Qibla direction are calculated on your device, and your location and preferences never reach the developer. The application has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. The only information that leaves your device is an approximate location, or a city name you search for, sent to Apple Maps to look up place names.

Introduction

Nur is a minimalist Islamic prayer times and Qibla direction application for iOS. This Privacy Policy describes how the application handles information when you use it. Prayer times, reminders and the Qibla direction are all calculated on your device, and the application does not operate any servers that collect or store your personal information.

Information We Collect

Location

If you choose to share it, Nur uses your device's location while the app is in use to calculate prayer times for your area and to determine the direction of the Qibla. Location access is optional: you can instead choose a city, either from a list built into the app or through an online search. Your location is not collected or stored by the developer.

So that prayer reminders stay up to date when the app is closed, Nur keeps your last known location on your device, rounded to about one kilometre. It is never transmitted to the developer.

Compass

Nur reads your device's compass heading to show when you are facing the Qibla. If Location Services are off, the heading is corrected for your chosen city using a magnetic model built into the app. Compass data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to any external server.

Preferences and Settings

The app stores your chosen settings locally on your device, including your prayer calculation method, Asr method, time format, per-prayer reminder and snooze settings, your selected city, and up to five recently selected cities. This data is stored using on-device storage and is not synchronized to any cloud service or transmitted to the developer.

Prayer Times Cache

The app calculates prayer times on your device and caches the current day's times locally, so they don't need to be recalculated each time the app opens. The cache is refreshed each day and whenever your location or settings change.

Local Notifications

If you enable prayer time reminders, Nur schedules local notifications on your device. iOS may briefly wake the app in the background to keep upcoming reminders accurate; this happens entirely on your device. Notification data is not sent to any external service.

How We Use Your Information

Your location is used solely to calculate your daily prayer times, to determine the Qibla direction, and to show the name of your city. Your stored preferences are used to present the app in the way you have configured it. No information is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond the core functionality of the application.

Third-Party Services

Nur uses one external service, Apple Maps, provided by Apple through iOS. Prayer times are not fetched from any server.

Apple Maps (MapKit)

When you use your device's location, Nur sends Apple an approximate location, rounded to about one kilometre, to show the name of your city. When you choose a city that needs it, Nur also uses Apple Maps to look up that city's time zone. If you search for a city online, the name you typed is sent to Apple Maps, but only when you tap Search. Suggestions from the city list built into the app never leave your device. Apple's handling of these requests is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Nur sends these requests through Apple's built-in iOS services, without any account or identifier belonging to the developer. No other external services receive any data from Nur.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

Nur does not sell, trade, or otherwise share your information with third parties, except for the Apple Maps requests described above. The developer does not have access to your location, settings, or usage data.

Data Storage and Security

All data generated by your use of Nur is stored locally on your device using iOS on-device storage. There are no developer-operated servers, no databases, and no cloud infrastructure associated with this application. Your data is subject to your device's own security mechanisms, including your device passcode and iOS data protection.

Children's Privacy

Nur does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The application does not include any features designed to collect information from children, and no such information is transmitted to the developer.

Your Choices and Rights

You can remove recently selected cities at any time from the app's Location settings, and turn prayer reminders off from its Notifications settings. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data from your device. Because the developer does not receive or store your personal information, there is no additional data to request deletion of.

You can revoke the app's access to your location or notifications at any time in your device's Settings. Without location access, you can keep using the app by choosing a city, and the Qibla direction continues to work.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised version will be published in this repository with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue in the GitHub repository for this project.
