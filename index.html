Nur

Privacy Policy

Effective April 6, 2026

Nur does not collect, store, or have access to your personal information. Your location, device data, and preferences never leave your device or reach the developer. The application has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. It is built to work for you, privately, on your device.

Introduction

Nur is a minimalist Islamic prayer times and Qibla direction application for iOS. This Privacy Policy describes how the application handles information when you use it. The vast majority of data processing occurs on your device, and the application does not operate any servers that collect or store your personal information.

Information We Collect

Location

Nur requests access to your device's location while the app is in use. Your geographic coordinates are used to calculate accurate prayer times for your area and to determine the direction of the Qibla. You may also provide a location manually through the app's search feature, in which case the search query you enter is used in place of your GPS coordinates. Your location is not collected or stored by the developer.

Device Motion and Compass

Nur accesses your device's motion sensors and compass heading to display the Qibla compass. This data is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to any external server.

Preferences and Settings

The app stores your chosen settings locally on your device, including your prayer calculation method, Asr school preference, time format, per-prayer notification settings, time adjustments, display preferences, and any manually saved location. This data is stored using on-device storage and is not synchronized to any cloud service or transmitted to the developer.

Prayer Times Cache

To reduce unnecessary network requests, the app caches your calculated prayer times locally on your device for the current day. This cache is automatically refreshed each day and when the app returns to the foreground.

Local Notifications

If you enable prayer time reminders, Nur schedules local notifications on your device. All notification data remains on your device and is not sent to any external service.

How We Use Your Information

Your location is used solely to calculate your daily prayer times and to determine Qibla direction. Your stored preferences are used to present the app in the way you have configured it. No information is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond the core functionality of the application.

Third-Party Services

Nur communicates with two external services to perform its core functions.

Aladhan API — api.aladhan.com

Receives your latitude, longitude, and selected calculation method in order to return your daily prayer times. You can review the Aladhan API's privacy practices at their website.

OpenStreetMap Nominatim — nominatim.openstreetmap.org

Used for reverse geocoding — converting your coordinates into a readable location name — and for the location search feature. When you search for a location by name, your search query is sent to this service. Nominatim is operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation; you can review their privacy policy on their website.

Neither service requires an account or API key, which means neither service has a mechanism to associate requests with an individual user. No other external services receive any data from Nur.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

Nur does not sell, trade, or otherwise share your information with third parties, except as described above for the two API services required for the app to function. The developer does not have access to your location, settings, or usage data.

Data Storage and Security

All data generated by your use of Nur is stored locally on your device using iOS on-device storage. There are no developer-operated servers, no databases, and no cloud infrastructure associated with this application. Your data is subject to your device's own security mechanisms, including your device passcode and iOS data protection.

Children's Privacy

Nur does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The application does not include any features designed to collect information from children, and no such information is transmitted to the developer.

Your Choices and Rights

You may clear all locally stored settings and preferences at any time through the app's settings screen. Uninstalling the app will remove all locally stored data from your device. Because the developer does not receive or store your personal information, there is no additional data to request deletion of.

You may revoke the app's access to your location or motion sensors at any time through your iOS device settings. If location access is revoked, you may continue to use the app by entering a location manually.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised version will be published in this repository with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue in the GitHub repository for this project.
